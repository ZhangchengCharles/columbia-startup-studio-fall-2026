# Session 1

**Date:** November 8, 2026  
**Participant:** P1  
**Who they are:** 18-year-old high school senior preparing for an SAT retake. Often opens Instagram while doing homework. Uses an iPhone; has never created a Shortcuts automation.  
**How we found them:** Introduced through a friend (the friend's younger sibling)  
**Stranger or someone we know?** Acquaintance  
**Consent form signed?** Yes  
**Recording consent?** No (written notes only)  
**Recording link:** None, participant declined recording  
**Moderator:** Team member A  
**Note-taker:** Team member B  
**Session length:** 17 minutes

#### Task-by-task observations

##### Task 1: Sign up

- **What they did:** Opened Sign in and paused at "Welcome back." Looked around the page before noticing "Create an account." Entered their details, checked the eight-character password requirement, and completed registration.
- **What they said out loud:** "I thought I was on the wrong page because I don't have an account yet."
- **Completed?** Yes, without assistance

##### Task 2: Set up the automation

- **What they did:** Selected Instagram and kept the default "At most once an hour." Copied the link and opened Shortcuts. Switched between the instructions and Shortcuts several times. Got stuck when the If action showed "File Size" instead of "Anything." Needed the moderator to point them back to the relevant instruction.
- **What they said out loud:** "I thought I just had to paste the link. What is this file part for?"
- **Completed?** With trouble (one moderator prompt)

##### Task 3: One practice question and its explanation (aha moment)

- **What they did:** Answered 40 on the question asking for the product of all solutions of |x − 3| = 7. Read the explanation and noticed one solution was −4. Left the optional mistake tags blank, then tapped "Back to Instagram."
- **What they said out loud:** "Oh, I forgot one of the answers could be negative."
- **Completed?** Yes

**Hesitations:** Paused on the "Welcome back" sign-in screen before finding "Create an account."

**Wrong clicks:** Entered through Sign in instead of account creation. In Shortcuts, left the If action on "File Size" instead of "Anything."

**Questions out loud:** "What is this file part for?" Asked whether the temporary file needed to be downloaded.

**Dropped tasks:** None. Said they probably would have stopped during setup if testing alone.

**Workarounds:** None observed.

**Did they reach the aha moment?** Yes, around minute 12, after reading why the product was −40.

#### Post-session answers

1. What does it do? "Gives me a quick SAT question before I start scrolling."
2. When did it click? "When I got one wrong and it explained the mistake right away."
3. Most confusing part? "Making the shortcut, especially the file part."
4. Anything missing? "A short video showing exactly what to tap."
5. Would you use it again? "Yeah, once it's already set up."
6. Magic wand? "Make setup one button."

#### Quick insight

The biggest thing this session showed us was that the practice experience made sense quickly, but the unfamiliar Shortcuts instructions nearly stopped the tester from reaching it.
